We extract all five standard resolution variations provided by YouTube's image CDN:
  • Ultra HD (maxresdefault.jpg): 1280 × 720 px
  • Standard Definition (sddefault.jpg): 640 × 480 px
  • High Quality (hqdefault.jpg): 480 × 360 px
  • Medium Quality (mqdefault.jpg): 320 × 180 px
  • Normal / Small (default.jpg): 120 × 90 px
All images are provided in standard JPEG format, ensuring universal compatibility with all operating systems and editing software.
YouTube only generates a maxresdefault.jpg file if the video creator uploaded a custom thumbnail that was at least 1280x720 pixels. If the creator chose an auto-selected frame or uploaded a low-resolution graphic, YouTube will not have a maxres version. In these instances, simply download the Standard (SD) or High Quality (HQ) versions.
